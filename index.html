Who We Are

Three generations of hands in the soil.

What began as a single 40-acre plot has grown into 1,200 acres of vegetables, fruit orchards, grain fields, poultry houses and grazing land. Every crop we plant and every animal we raise is grown under the same principle our founders started with: care for the land, and the land will feed generations.

Today GreenHarvest Farms supplies fresh, honestly grown produce to families, restaurants, supermarkets and export partners — without cutting corners on how it's grown.

"Our mission is simple: grow food the right way, and get it to your table while it's still fresh." — GreenHarvest Founding Family
