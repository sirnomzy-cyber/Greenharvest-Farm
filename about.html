Our Story

From one field to a full agribusiness.

GreenHarvest Farms began in 1999 when the Adeyemi family cleared a single 40-acre plot to grow vegetables for their local community. What started as a modest family farm grew, season after season, into 1,200 acres spanning vegetable fields, fruit orchards, grain plots, poultry houses and grazing pasture.

Along the way, the principle never changed: grow food properly, treat the land as a long-term partner, and never let scale compromise quality. That same philosophy now guides every one of our 180 full-time farmworkers, agronomists and logistics staff.

Our Mission

To grow and deliver fresh, honestly farmed food that households, restaurants and businesses can depend on — while protecting the soil, water and communities we farm alongside.

Our Vision

To become the region's most trusted farm-to-market supplier — known equally for the freshness of our produce and the integrity of how it's grown.

How We Farm

Modern equipment, sustainable practices.

Precision planting equipment and drip irrigation reduce water use while improving yield consistency across every field. Soil is tested each season, and crop rotation keeps our land productive without depending on synthetic inputs.

  • Drip Irrigation

    Targeted watering cuts water use by up to 40% versus flood irrigation.

  • Soil Health Monitoring

    Seasonal soil testing keeps nutrients balanced without over-fertilizing.

  • Mechanized Planting

    Precision seeders reduce waste and improve germination rates.
